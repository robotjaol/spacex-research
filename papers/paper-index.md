# Paper and technical-method catalogue

Journal papers, conference papers, technical reports and manuals are labeled separately. Older foundational methods are retained because the task concerns engineering principles, not only recent publications.

* [Successive Convexification for 6-DoF Mars Rocket Powered Landing with Free-Final-Time](https://arxiv.org/html/1802.03827v1) (2018), AIAA conference author manuscript. Use: Advanced constrained-guidance project; independent trajectory integration. Review/limitations: Full HTML reviewed; AIAA 2018-0617; DOI 10.2514/6.2018-0617. Negligible aerodynamic forces and simplified rigid-body assumptions. The starter PD controller is not this algorithm.
* [Generalized hp Pseudospectral Convex Programming for Powered Descent and Landing](https://elib.dlr.de/118313/1/Generalized_hp_pseudospectral_convex_algorithm_for_powered_descent_and_landing.pdf) (2018), DLR-hosted AIAA manuscript. Use: Compare mesh refinement and independent integration. Review/limitations: 23-page PDF reviewed; AIAA 2018-1870; DOI 10.2514/6.2018-1870. The DLR landing-page title differs; use PDF metadata. Do not confuse with the 2019 journal article.
* [An Introduction to the Kalman Filter](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~pstone/Courses/393Rfall15/readings/Welch%2BBishop-TR-95.pdf) (2004), University technical report. Use: Synthetic position/velocity filtering and observation outages. Review/limitations: Full 16-page PDF; UNC TR 95-041, revision1 March 2004. Optimality depends on the assumed process and observation models. Original report number is from 1995.
* [Exploring the Internet from space with Hypatia](https://bdebopam.github.io/papers/imc2020-hypatia.pdf) (2020), ACM IMC author paper. Use: Geometry first; optional future packet-level scenarios. Review/limitations: Full 16-page PDF; DOI 10.1145/3419394.3423635. Historical constellation assumptions; geometry alone cannot estimate service throughput or RTT.
* [Computer Program for Calculation of Complex Chemical Equilibrium Compositions and Applications. Part 1: Analysis](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19950013764) (1994), NASA RP 1311 PartI. Use: Reference conditions for an optional thermochemistry module. Review/limitations: NASA publication listing verified; scanned mirror opens but detailed equations were not text-reviewed. Direct NTRS access returned403. No claim of a complete equation audit or Raptor reconstruction.
* [Computer Program for Calculation of Complex Chemical Equilibrium Compositions and Applications. II. Users Manual and Program Description](https://rocketcea.readthedocs.io/en/latest/_static/CEA_User_Manual_(NASA_RP-1311).pdf) (1996), NASA RP 1311 PartII, documentation mirror. Use: Reproduce selected AppendixG cases with recorded database versions. Review/limitations: 184-page mirrored PDF read; contents, introduction and input definitions inspected. Legacy1996 instructions do not describe current installation; database revisions affect output.
* [Liquid hydrogen tank boil-off model for design and optimization](https://mdolab.engin.umich.edu/bibliography/Adler2025b) (2025), Journal of Thermophysics and Heat Transfer. Use: Advanced successor to the sensible-heating starter; separate mass/energy checks. Review/limitations: University bibliography and linked34-page author manuscript reviewed; DOI 10.2514/1.T7005. Some experimental plot axes are suppressed; full raw-data reproduction is limited. Publication2025, prepublication2024.
* [The Power of 10: Rules for Developing Safety-Critical Code](https://spinroot.com/gerard/pdf/P10.pdf) (2006), Computer 39(6), author manuscript. Use: Failure-path tests and inspectable simulation code. Review/limitations: Six-page manuscript reviewed; DOI 10.1109/MC.2006.212. Primarily C guidance; neither a universal Python style nor certification.
* [MIT AVL User Primer: AVL3.36](https://web.mit.edu/drela/Public/web/avl/AVL_User_Primer.pdf) (undated), University software technical manual. Use: Low-speed lifting-surface mesh convergence studies. Review/limitations: 43-page manual reviewed; site dates release 3.36 to 2017 but PDF publication date is unclear. Subsonic and thin-surface assumptions do not validate hypersonic grid fins or bluff-body reentry.
* [2.080 Structural Mechanics Lecture4: Development of Constitutive Equations of Continuum, Beams and Plates](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-080j-structural-mechanics-fall-2013/32670f14cec210d98c5c7fe9dbf73eb6_MIT2_080JF13_Lecture4.pdf) (2013), Graduate lecture notes. Use: Analytic beam benchmark before finite-element models. Review/limitations: 19-page PDF and Fall 2013 course metadata read. Beam verification does not validate buckling, thermal stress, welds or a rocket structure.
* [Revisiting Spacetrack Report#3](https://celestrak.org/publications/AIAA/2006-6753/AIAA-2006-6753-Rev3.pdf) (2006), AIAA/AAS conference paper, revision3. Use: Separate advanced OMM/SGP4 regression study. Review/limitations: 94-page PDF reviewed; DOI 10.2514/6.2006-6753; verification appendix inspected. TLE mean elements are not direct two-body initial conditions; match reference frames and model assumptions.
* [The Byzantine Generals Problem](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/byz.pdf) (1982), ACM TOPLAS4(3), author paper. Use: Contrast distributed agreement with a centralized scalar sensor voter. Review/limitations: 20-page paper and Microsoft Research metadata reviewed; DOI 10.1145/357172.357176. A three-sensor median voter is not Byzantine consensus; common-mode and voter faults remain.
