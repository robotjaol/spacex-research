# Data and parameter provenance

The shipped CSV files are synthetic outputs, generated by `aero-lab all --seed 7`. They contain no flight telemetry and no scraped spacecraft metadata. Parameter values not listed below are educational scenario choices, not fitted or reverse-engineered SpaceX values.

| Input | Value / handling | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Earth gravitational parameter |398600.435507 km³/s², converted by 1e9 to m³/s²|[Astrodynamic Parameters](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/astro_par.html)|
| Mean Earth radius |6371.0084 km, converted by 1000 to m|[Planetary Physical Parameters](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/phys_par.html)|
| Sidereal day |86164.09054 s; constant rotation approximation|[Astrodynamic Parameters](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/astro_par.html)|
| Standard gravity in vertical exercise |9.80665 m/s²; adopted conventional constant|Scenario configuration|
| Satellite count, shell and station |72 synthetic satellites;550 km;53°; station−7.25°,112.75°|Chosen educational scenario, not Starlink's current fleet|
| Sensor noise / injected faults |Generator, seed and masks in source|Synthetic scenario|
| Thermal capacity / conductance |200000 J/K and 5 W/K|Abstract thermal body; not measured tank properties|

For a future live-orbit extension, use [A New Way to Obtain GP Data (aka TLEs)](https://celestrak.org/NORAD/documentation/gp-data-formats.php) and the SGP4 verification cases in [Revisiting Spacetrack Report#3](https://celestrak.org/publications/AIAA/2006-6753/AIAA-2006-6753-Rev3.pdf). Preserve object IDs, epoch, retrieval timestamp, provider terms and frame. Do not load mean elements into the present Cartesian two-body model. Data retrieval is an optional future adapter and is not implemented.

Published videos and regulatory filings can guide hypotheses but do not supply complete calibrated telemetry. No source established an openly licensed full Raptor dataset, internal flight-controller gains, manufacturing CAD or tank-test dataset suitable for complete SpaceX reconstruction.
